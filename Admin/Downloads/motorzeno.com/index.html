Frequently asked questions

Will checking my rate hurt my credit?

Initial rate checks use a soft inquiry and should not impact your credit score. A hard inquiry may occur only if you proceed with a lender.

What vehicles can I finance?

Most applicants finance new or used cars, trucks, and SUVs that meet lender guidelines for age, mileage, and condition.

How long does approval take?

Many customers receive initial options within minutes. Final funding timelines vary by lender and documentation.

Can I refinance my current auto loan?

Yes. If you want a lower payment or better rate, refinance options may be available based on your profile and remaining balance.
