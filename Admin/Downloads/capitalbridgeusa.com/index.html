Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Is CapitalBridgeUsa a lender?
No. CapitalBridgeUsa passes requests to lenders in our network. Any loan, rate, or term comes from the lender you are matched with, not from us.
Will asking hurt my credit?
Not on its own. A lender who follows up may run a credit check — that part is covered in our Credit Authorization page.
Is this available where I live?
Not in every state yet. Our Lending Policy page lists current availability.
How much can I request?
Between $100 and $5000. Whether a lender can offer an amount in that range is up to them.
My credit has seen better days — worth trying anyway?
Often, yes. Lenders in our network each set their own criteria, and some work with lower credit scores. There is no cost and no obligation to find out.
