Questions

Straight answers

>My credit is not great. Is it still worth trying?
Often, yes. Lenders in our network each set their own criteria, and some work with lower credit scores. There is no cost and no obligation to find out.
>Does submitting affect my credit?
Not on its own. A lender who follows up may run a credit check, covered in our Credit Authorization page.
>Is this available in my state?
Not everywhere. Current availability is listed on our Lending Policy page.
>Is Fundpinegrove a lender?
No. Fundpinegrove routes requests to lenders in our network. Any loan, rate, or term comes from the lender you are matched with.
>What does this cost?
Submitting a request is free. Any fees tied to an offer you accept come from the lender and are disclosed up front.

Important disclosures

01

Fundpinegrove is not a lender. Requests are routed to lenders in our network, who decide independently whether to respond. Submitting a request is not a guarantee of being matched or approved.

02

Fundpinegrove may be compensated by a lender in our network for a successful connection. This has no effect on you and is never billed to you. See our Advertising Disclosure.

03

A lender who follows up may check your credit or verify details like income or banking before making an offer. See Credit Authorization.

04

Short-term borrowing can cost more if it is not repaid on schedule. Review the complete terms of any offer before accepting.

05

This service isn't available in every state. Our Lending Policy has current state availability, and Rates & Fees explains how rates and terms actually work once you're matched with a lender.
