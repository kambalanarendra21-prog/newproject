Questions, answered

Still unsure about something?

These cover what people usually ask before requesting.

1Is this available in my state?
Not universally. The Lending Policy page lists where this currently isn't available, worth a quick look first.
2Will sending a request affect my credit?
Not on its own. If a lender you're connected with runs a credit check, that's their process, see Credit Authorization for details.
3How much can I actually request?
Anywhere from $200 to $5000. Whether you're offered anything in that range is up to the lender's own criteria.
4Does it cost anything to send a request?
No. If you accept an offer, any fees tied to it are the lender's and will be shown to you before you agree to anything.
5Does FinraLabs lend money directly?
No. FinraLabs routes your request to lenders in our network, approval, rate, and terms are entirely their call, never ours.
