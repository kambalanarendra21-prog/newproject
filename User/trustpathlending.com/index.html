Questions

Straight answers

Is this available where I live?
Not in every state yet. Our Lending Policy page has current availability.
What does it cost?
Nothing to send a request. Any fees tied to an offer you accept come from the lender and are spelled out before you agree to anything.
Is Trustpathlending a lender?
No. Trustpathlending routes requests to lenders in our network. Any loan, rate, or term comes from the lender you're matched with, not from us.
My credit has some marks — still worth trying?
Often, yes. Lenders in our network each set their own bar, and some work with lower credit scores. No cost, no obligation to find out.
How much can I ask for?
Between $200 and $5000. Whether a lender can offer that amount is up to them.

Important disclosures

01

Trustpathlending is not a lender. Requests are routed to lenders in our network, who decide independently whether to respond. Submitting a request is not a guarantee of being matched or approved.

02

Trustpathlending may be compensated by a lender in our network for a successful connection. This has no effect on you and is never billed to you. See our Advertising Disclosure.

03

A lender who follows up may check your credit or verify details like income or banking before making an offer. See Credit Authorization.

04

A short-term loan is built for a temporary gap, not routine expenses. Ask only for what you can repay, and read the full terms before agreeing.

05

This service isn't available in every state. Our Lending Policy has current state availability, and Rates & Fees explains how rates and terms actually work once you're matched with a lender.
