Questions

A few things people ask

How much can I ask for?
Between $200 and $5000. Whether a lender can offer an amount in that range is entirely up to them.
Does it cost anything to ask?
No. Any fees tied to an offer you accept would come from the lender, and they'll walk you through those before you agree to anything.
Is this available where I live?
Not everywhere just yet. Current availability is listed on our Lending Policy page.
Is Lendinganchor a lender?
No — Lendinganchor passes your request along to lenders in our network. Any approval, rate, or term comes from whichever lender responds, not from us.
Will this affect my credit?
Asking doesn't, by itself. If a lender you're connected with checks your credit, that's covered in our Credit Authorization page.

Important disclosures

01

To be clear: Lendinganchor doesn't lend money or set terms. We simply connect your request with lenders in our network. Submitting a request is not a guarantee of being matched or approved.

02

Lendinganchor may be compensated by a lender in our network for a successful connection — this has no effect on you and is never billed to you. See our Advertising Disclosure.

03

Once you're connected, a lender may look at your credit or verify what you've shared. See Credit Authorization.

04

A short-term loan is meant for a temporary need, not everyday expenses. Ask only for what feels manageable, and read every term before agreeing.

05

This service isn't available in every state. Our Lending Policy has current state availability, and Rates & Fees explains how rates and terms actually work once you're matched with a lender.
