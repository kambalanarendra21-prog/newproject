Questions, answered

Still unsure about something?

These cover what people usually ask before requesting.

Is Lendbrancher actually the one lending me money?
No — Lendbrancher passes your request to lenders in our network. Whichever one reaches out to you is the one deciding on approval, rate, and terms, not us.
What do lenders in your network typically look for?
It varies by lender, but the basics most look for are: you're 18 or older, a U.S. resident in a state where this is offered, have an active bank account, and some form of regular income. Meeting those doesn't guarantee an offer — each lender still decides on its own — but it's usually the starting point.
How much can I ask for?
Anywhere from $100 to $5000. What you're actually offered, if anyone offers, is up to that lender's own criteria — we don't set it.
Will this show up on my credit report?
Sending a request through Lendbrancher on its own doesn't touch your score. If a lender you're matched with runs a credit check, that's their process — see Credit Authorization for how that works.
Can I even use this where I live?
Depends on your state. The Lending Policy page has the current list of places this isn't available yet — worth a quick check first.

Important disclosures

Not a lender

Lendbrancher is a marketing and referral service, not a lender. We don't make lending decisions, broker loans, or act on behalf of any lender. Submitting a request is not a guarantee of being matched or approved.

How we're paid

We may receive compensation from the lenders in our network for referring your request to them — you never pay for this, and it doesn't affect whether any lender decides to approve you. See our Advertising Disclosure.

Credit checks

A lender you're connected with may run a credit check or verify information you've provided (identity, income, banking) as part of deciding whether to make an offer. See Credit Authorization.

Borrow responsibly

A short-term loan is best used for a one-time need, not repeat borrowing. Whatever offer you receive, take the time to read it in full and walk away from anything you can't comfortably repay.

Where this works

This service isn't available in every state. Our Lending Policy has current state availability, and Rates & Fees explains how rates and terms actually work once you're matched with a lender.
