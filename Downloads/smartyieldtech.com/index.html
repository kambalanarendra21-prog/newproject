Questions, answered

Still unsure about something?

These cover what people usually ask before requesting.

Does submitting a request cost me anything?
No. Sending a request through Smartyieldtech is free. If you do accept a loan, whatever fees come with it are the lender's, and they'll show you those before you agree to anything.
Is Smartyieldtech actually the one lending me money?
No — Smartyieldtech passes your request to lenders in our network. Whichever one reaches out to you is the one deciding on approval, rate, and terms, not us.
My credit isn't great, or I've been turned down elsewhere — is this worth trying?
Possibly — lenders in our network set their own criteria, and some do consider applicants with less-than-perfect credit. We can't promise you'll be matched, but sending a request through Smartyieldtech is free and doesn't commit you to anything.
Will this show up on my credit report?
Sending a request through Smartyieldtech on its own doesn't touch your score. If a lender you're matched with runs a credit check, that's their process — see Credit Authorization for how that works.
How much can I ask for?
Anywhere from $100 to $5000. What you're actually offered, if anyone offers, is up to that lender's own criteria — we don't set it.

Important disclosures

Not a lender

To be direct: Smartyieldtech doesn't lend money or decide who gets approved. We connect requests to lenders — everything past that point is between you and them. Submitting a request is not a guarantee of being matched or approved.

How we're paid

Smartyieldtech may be paid by the lenders in our network when we pass along a request. That doesn't cost you anything extra, and it has no bearing on whether you're actually matched or approved. See our Advertising Disclosure.

Credit checks

A lender you're connected with may run a credit check or verify information you've provided (identity, income, banking) as part of deciding whether to make an offer. See Credit Authorization.

Borrow responsibly

A short-term loan is best used for a one-time need, not repeat borrowing. Whatever offer you receive, take the time to read it in full and walk away from anything you can't comfortably repay.

Where this works

This service isn't available in every state. Our Lending Policy has current state availability, and Rates & Fees explains how rates and terms actually work once you're matched with a lender.
