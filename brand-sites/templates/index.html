What happens after you send it

  1. You describe the request

    Amount and a way to reach you. That is the entire first step.

  2. {{ brand.name }} passes it along

    Independent lenders who cover your state can review the request. {{ brand.name }} does not pick a winner or promise a match.

  3. A lender may reply

    If someone wants to continue, they contact you with their own terms — or they take you to their site.

  4. You accept or you leave

    Read every number before you agree. If the offer does not fit, decline it.

Typical starting points

Most lenders in the network look for an adult U.S. resident in a state they serve, a working bank account, and regular income. Clearing those items is not an approval.

Common questions

Is {{ brand.name }} a lender?
No. It only forwards requests. Cash, rates, and yes-or-no decisions come from the lender you hear from.
Does asking cost money?
No. Fees, if any, belong to a loan you later accept, and that lender must show them first.
Will this change my credit score?
Sending this page does not, by itself. A lender who continues may run a credit check. See the disclosures.
Is this available where I live?
Not in every state. If no lender in the network can serve your location, you will not be matched.
What amounts can I request?
$100 to $5,000. Whether anyone offers that amount is up to the lender.
